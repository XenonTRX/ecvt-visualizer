Power Split — where the engine's watts actually go

_□✕

Engine power leaves the carrier by two routes at once: straight through the gears to the ring, and through MG1 as electricity. The sign of MG1's speed is the sign of the electrical flow. Once the output turns fast enough relative to the engine, MG1 goes negative, starts motoring, and MG2 has to generate to feed it — the gears then carry more power than the engine makes.

Engine Map — torque, speed and power

_□✕

Dashed hyperbolae are lines of constant power — torque times speed. The blue curve is the engine's efficient operating line. Because MG1 can set engine speed independently of road speed, the controller can keep the engine sitting on that curve at any vehicle speed. That is the entire point of the e-CVT.

The Relationships

_□✕
Speeds
Zₛ·ωMG1 + Zᵣ·ωout = (Zₛ+Zᵣ)·ωICE
ωMG1 = 3.6·ωICE − 2.6·ωout
ωICE = 0.2778·ωMG1 + 0.7222·ωout
  • Two degrees of freedom. Fix any two speeds and the third is forced.
  • Mechanical point: ωMG1 = 0 at ωout/ωICE = 3.6/2.6 = 1.3846.
  • No reverse gear exists. Reverse is MG2 spinning backwards with the engine off.
Torques — fixed fractions, at every speed
T MG1 = Zₛ/(Zₛ+Zᵣ) · T ICE = 0.2778 · T ICE
T ring = Zᵣ/(Zₛ+Zᵣ) · T ICE = 0.7222 · T ICE
T out = 0.7222 · T ICE + T MG2
  • MG1 can only ever hold back 27.8 % of engine torque. Everything else the wheels feel comes from MG2.
  • MG1's torque is the clutch. Set it to zero and the whole gearset carries nothing, which is how the engine disconnects in EV mode — there is no clutch pack anywhere in the box.
Power
P mech = 0.7222 · T ICE · ωout P elec = 0.2778 · T ICE · ωMG1
P mech + P elec = P ICE
f elec = 1 − 0.7222 · ωout / ωICE
battery neutral ⇒ T out = T ICE · ωICE / ωout
  • The electric fraction depends only on the speed ratio, and it is a straight line.
  • With the battery neither charging nor discharging, MG1 and MG2 together behave as a perfect CVT of ratio ωICE/ωout. The torque ratio goes to infinity at standstill, which is why a Toyota hybrid needs no torque converter and no launch clutch.
  • Below the mechanical point the electric fraction goes negative: power circulates backwards through two conversions, each about 90 % efficient. That is the high-speed cruise penalty.
